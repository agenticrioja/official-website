# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Local people curious about AI agents and practitioners looking for peers and meetups are equally important. Visitors may be developers, researchers, or other curious people in Logroño and La Rioja. Newcomers should be able to participate without prior expertise.

## Product Purpose

Agentic Rioja brings people together in person to share what they are building with AI agents, learn from one another, and form local connections. Over the next year, success means meetup attendance and active connections among local participants.

## Positioning

A friendly, vendor-neutral AI agents community rooted in Logroño, focused on real work and open conversation. It is the official Logroño chapter of the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF), a Linux Foundation project.

## Operating Context

The site introduces the community and its topics, explains what a meetup is like, lists upcoming and past events, and directs people to Luma to join. People can participate by attending, giving a talk, or offering a venue. Cloud Native Rioja is a sibling community.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static, single-page English website built with Astro and deployed to GitHub Pages. The main copy stays in English; small Spanish touches are welcome.
- Event details and organizer biographies come from the organizers. Dates include their timezone offset. Upcoming and past events are determined at build time, and the site rebuilds daily.
- Keep AAIF identity and links, the Linux Foundation mention and Code of Conduct, and the Cloud Native Rioja link visible.
- Do not add analytics, trackers, or third-party scripts without the organizers' approval. Do not publish personal email addresses or private source materials.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Agentic Rioja. The voice is friendly, local, and professional: a community rather than a corporation. AAIF brand assets and the Cloud Native Rioja identity are present in the repository.

## Evidence on Hand

- Confirmed event details: `src/content/events/`.
- Organizer supplied profiles: `src/content/organizers/`.
- AAIF, community, and people assets: `src/assets/`.
- There are no confirmed testimonials, impact figures, or attendance metrics to publish.

## Product Principles

1. Welcome curiosity and hands-on experience with equal care.
2. Help visitors find and attend local meetups.
3. Make it easy for people to connect and contribute in person.
4. Show real community work without inventing people, event details, or outcomes.
5. Keep the AAIF affiliation and local identity clear.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must meet WCAG 2.1 AA. Motion assets show a still frame when reduced motion is requested. Participation is open to beginners as well as experienced practitioners.
